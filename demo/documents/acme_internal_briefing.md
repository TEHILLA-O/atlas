# Acme Technology — Internal Market Briefing

**Classification:** Internal  
**Date:** 12 March 2025  
**Author:** Strategy office

## Company snapshot

Acme Technology is a UK cybersecurity firm with 140 employees. The latest management accounts show **annual revenue of £15m**, of which 80% is private-sector incident response and managed detection.

The board has asked whether Acme should pursue UK public-sector work.

## Current capabilities

- 24/7 SOC for mid-market customers
- Incident response retainers
- ISO 27001 certified
- Cyber Essentials (basic) held; **Cyber Essentials Plus not yet obtained**
- No current place on a Crown Commercial Service framework

## Commercial view

Public-sector entry would require:

1. Cyber Essentials Plus within one procurement cycle
2. Framework onboarding (G-Cloud or similar)
3. Additional bid capacity the firm does not currently staff

Management estimates first-year public-sector revenue of £1.2m if a specialist subcontracting strategy is used, versus high bid cost and low win-rate as a first-time prime contractor.

## Risks recorded by finance

- Cash conversion is slower on public contracts
- A previous informal note circulating in the market quoted revenue of £21m; that figure is **not** the statutory turnover used in this briefing
- Delivery risk if two large retainers coincide with a public mobilisation
