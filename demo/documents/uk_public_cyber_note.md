# UK Public-Sector Cybersecurity Market Note

**Source type:** Industry briefing (illustrative demo document)  
**Audience:** Corporate development

## Market

UK public bodies continue to buy cybersecurity through frameworks rather than one-off tenders. Specialist suppliers typically enter as subcontractors to incumbents before bidding as primes.

Procurement teams commonly require:

- Cyber Essentials Plus
- Evidence of incident-response retainers
- ISO 27001 or equivalent

## Competitive landscape

Incumbents include large systems integrators and established MSSPs. New mid-market specialists rarely win large prime contracts in the first year.

## Opportunity pattern

The strongest near-term pattern is niche supply:

- SOC overflow
- Incident response surge
- Departmental advisory for smaller public bodies

Barriers are accreditation, framework membership, and referenceability — not a lack of demand.
